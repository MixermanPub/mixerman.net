<div class="lineband lineband--thin"><h2 class="plate">An Accurate Room</h2></div>

<img class="room-photo" src="/uploads/2025/08/Mix-Room.webp" alt="Mixerman's mix and production suite in Asheville, NC — tapestried walls, string lights, and monitors at the mix position" width="941" height="941">

I built my mix and production room to the Golden Ratio. My studio is acoustically tuned and features Dutch & Dutch 8c monitors that adapt to the room. This allows me to deliver fully mastered mixes with perfect accuracy and predictable translation. From the very first bounce, you’re hearing your record as it’s meant to sound, with no guessing about how it will translate later.
