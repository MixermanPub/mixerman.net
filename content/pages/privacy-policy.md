**Privacy Policy**

**Effective Date:** September 22, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website.

## **Information We Collect**

We do not collect personal information from visitors to our website unless you voluntarily provide it, such as by contacting us through an email link or signing up for a newsletter.

## **Cookies and Tracking**

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and to see which pages people actually read. It sets cookies in your browser and collects information that does not identify you, such as:

*   Browser type
*   Device type
*   Approximate geographic location
*   Date and time of visit
*   Which pages you read, how far down them you get, and which links you follow off the site

That is the whole of it. We use it to work out what is worth writing more of. We do not learn your name or your email address from it, and we do not try to work out who you are. Google's own handling of what it collects is governed by Google's privacy policy.

If you would rather not be counted, Google publishes a browser add-on that opts you out of Google Analytics on every site that uses it: [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

A few posts embed a player from another service, such as a YouTube video or a podcast episode. The YouTube player loads in its privacy-enhanced mode, which sets no cookies until you press play. When you play any embedded media, the service hosting it may set its own cookies, governed by its own privacy policy.

## **Third-Party Links**

If you click on a link to a third-party website, such as a store or external platform (e.g., BookFunnel), their privacy policy will govern your interactions on those sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party websites.

## **Affiliate Links**

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. The Amazon buttons and links on this site carry my Associates tag. When you follow one and buy something, Amazon pays me a small commission. It costs you nothing, and the price is the same as it would be without the link. Amazon may set its own cookies when you arrive there, governed by Amazon's privacy policy. I do not learn who you are or what you bought.

## **Future Updates**

If we begin offering products or services directly through this website, we will update this Privacy Policy to reflect any changes in how your data is collected, used, and protected.

## **How to Contact Us**

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: mixerman@mixerman.net

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
