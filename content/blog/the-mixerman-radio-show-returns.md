Great news! As I was migrating to the new website I found a treasure trove of the old Mixerman Radio Shows including some of the early episodes that I thought were lost.

Since I get asked about these old shows all the time, I decided to put them up for anyone who wants to listen to them. Slippy is no longer with us, of course. I miss him dearly as do many of you. At least now we can all go and listen to his hysterical rants and missives when we’re thinking about him.

There’s lots of relevant information [here](/mixerman-radio-show/) for anyone who produces music, and there’s a lot of history too. The shows are certainly entertaining. So, if you want to take a walk down memory lane, they’re here for you to listen to any time you like.

Without further ado, I present to you...

[The Mixerman Radio Show, with Slipperman and Aardvark.](/mixerman-radio-show/)

Enjoy,<br />
Mixerman
