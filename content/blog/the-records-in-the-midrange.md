Where it comes to music and sound design, it’s important to understand where the fundamental frequencies lie. The top note of a violin is 4kHz. Yet people often automatically boost the high frequency information for purposes of clarity. But is that really the best approach? Maybe not. In this video, I demonstrate precisely why you may want to focus your attention on the midrange.
