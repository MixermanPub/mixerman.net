Larry Crane, Editor at Large for TapeOp Magazine, serves up his thoughts on my newest book MIXERMAN [_Musician’s_ _Survival_ _Guide to a Killer Record._](https://amzn.to/2NETK3y)

<a class="float-right" href="https://tapeop.com/reviews/gear/131/musicians-survival-guide-to-a-killer-record-book/"><img src="/uploads/2022/09/msg2kr-300px-wide.jpeg" alt="Musician's Survival Guide to a Killer Record — book cover" loading="lazy" /></a>

> “I’ve chatted with Eric “Mixerman” Sarafin in the past [_Tape Op_ [#34](https://tapeop.com/issues/34)] about his hilarious [_The Daily Adventures of Mixerman_](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-daily-adventures-of-mixerman/id1638184428) book and reviewed some of his helpful [_Zen and the Art of…_](/books/zen-the-art-of-mixing-mix-music-like-a-pro/) guides to producing, mixing, and recording. With _Musician’s Survival Guide to a Killer Record_, Mixerman turns the tables around and provides a guide for _musicians_ who are recording. The main gist of this book, and what separates it from most every tome meant for the self-recording musician, is that _Survival Guide_ is an overview on how to keep one’s eye on the real prize of making a recording to be proud of forever.”
