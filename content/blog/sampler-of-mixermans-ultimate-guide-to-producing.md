That’s right. I’ve written a new book and it’s called _MIXERMAN’S Ultimate Guide to PRODUCING Records, Music & Songs_.

The premise of the book is simple: these days, everyone’s a producer, and now you need to learn it all. Given this, I rewrote, revised, and greatly expanded my 2012 book _Zen and the Art of Producing_ until it covered the needs of everyone, whether you produce your own music at home or work for-hire in a studio.

Clocking in at nearly 600 pages, this is easily the most comprehensive book I’ve ever written on producing, recording, mixing and mastering. The sampler will give you a good taste of it.

If you’d rather skip the sampler and go straight to the book, you’ll find everything that’s inside, and all the places to get your copy, on [the book page](/books/mm-ultimate-producing/).
