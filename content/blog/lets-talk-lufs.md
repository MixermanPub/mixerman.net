If you believe that your mix at -7 LUFS will sound significantly better than at -14 LUFS, despite the level matching that streaming sites implement, this video is for you.

I mastered my mix of Krave Amiko’s “Oh So Badly” at -14, -10, and -7 LUFS, and then I level matched them (just as the streaming sites do) and I cut them all together for you to hear for yourself just how much of an advantage (or disadvantage) loudness offers you.

Enjoy,<br />
Mixerman
