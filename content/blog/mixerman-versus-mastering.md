I join Ian Shepherd and Jon Tidey on their podcast The Mastering Show. We had a lengthy discussion on mastering, in which I lay out a number of arguments as to why many people should consider automated Mastering, certainly through the early stages of their development. I also discuss how (and why) I’ve taken to integrating a brick wall limiter into my mixes, among many other things. If you wonder about mastering as it relates to you and your music, this is a discussion worth far more than the price of admission.

Enjoy,<br />
Mixerman
